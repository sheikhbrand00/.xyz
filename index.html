About Me

I'm Alex Morgan, a creative developer based in San Francisco with a passion for building beautiful, functional web experiences. With over 5 years of experience in frontend development and UI/UX design, I specialize in creating modern applications using React, TypeScript, and Node.js.

My approach combines technical expertise with design thinking. I believe that great software should not only work flawlessly but also feel intuitive and delightful to use. I'm particularly interested in accessibility, performance optimization, and creating inclusive digital experiences.
